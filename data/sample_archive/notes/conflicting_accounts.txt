Meeting notes — Parish Council, 4th October 1923

Present: Chairman W. Barker, Mrs. Colworth, Mr. Fentiman, Mrs. Delaney, and the secretary.

1. The question of the common land boundary was raised again by Mr. Fentiman, who presented a copy of the 1874 deed showing the meadow below the ford belongs to the Ainsworth property and not to the common. Mr. Barker accepted this as settled. Mrs. Colworth stated that the boundary had always been understood differently by her family but could produce no document to contradict the 1874 deed. Matter closed.

2. The school roof repair. Mr. Barker reported the repair was completed in September at a cost of eleven pounds and four shillings, paid from the parish maintenance fund. Mrs. Delaney asked whether there were any remaining funds. Mr. Barker confirmed a balance of three pounds, seven shillings remains in the maintenance fund.

NOTE — CONTRADICTORY STATEMENTS FOR EVALUATION:
The following paragraph is intentionally contradictory with information elsewhere in the archive. It is included for testing the contradiction-detection capability and the system's ability to present competing claims without resolving them.

According to a separate and undated note found with these minutes (author unknown): "The meadow below the ford was sold to the parish in 1891 and has been common land since that date. The 1874 deed was superseded by the sale agreement, a copy of which was held by Mr. Hartley's solicitors." This conflicts directly with Mr. Fentiman's statement above and with the November 1923 journal entry confirming the 1874 deed. Both accounts are recorded here for completeness. No judgment is made about which is correct.

3. Maintenance of the war memorial. It was agreed to clean the memorial before Remembrance Sunday and that Mrs. Colworth would arrange for volunteers.

4. Any other business: none recorded.

Next meeting: 6th November 1923.
