# Field Notes — Moorland Botanical Survey
## Conducted near Thornfield, Derbyshire, Summer 1886

### Purpose

These notes record plant specimens collected during walks across the moorland north and east of Thornfield House, with particular attention to flowering species not commonly catalogued for this area. Specimens were pressed and labelled; this notebook is the primary descriptive record.

### Site description

The moorland in question lies at approximately 280–320 metres elevation. The soil is predominantly peaty with patches of exposed millstone grit. Drainage is poor on the higher ground; several small boggy depressions exist near the western slope.

### Specimen records

**Specimen 1 — Greywing Pond margin, 14 July 1886**
Small-leafed plant, trailing habit, found in the damp margin of Greywing Pond on the east side. Purple flowers, five petals, approximately 8mm across. Leaves alternate, ovate, with a finely toothed edge. No obvious match in Bentham's handbook; referred for expert identification. Dr. Ashworth of the Thornfield Medical Practice examined this specimen on 22 July and agreed it warranted further attention. He has forwarded a pressed example to Dr. Langton at the Royal Botanical Society with a covering letter dated 25 July 1886.

**Specimen 2 — North ridge, 21 July 1886**
Common heather (Calluna vulgaris), abundant. Several examples pressed for completeness of record. Found at 310 metres on well-drained grit substrate.

**Specimen 3 — Boggy depression below the shepherd's wall, 3 August 1886**
Sundew (Drosera rotundifolia), confirmed. Several plants in good condition, actively catching small flies. This species is known from similar habitats in the county. Leaves basal, round, covered in red-tipped adhesive hairs. No specimens taken; observed and recorded only.

**Specimen 4 — Greywing Pond margin (second visit), 18 August 1886**
Return visit to confirm the purple-flowering specimen from 14 July. Found three additional plants of the same type. Flowers now slightly past peak. Measured leaf and stem dimensions carefully: stem 4–6 cm, leaf 1.2–1.8 cm length. Detailed sketch included on the adjacent page.

### Notes on Dr. Ashworth's interest

Dr. Ashworth has been encouraging about this survey. He mentioned that systematic botanical records for this particular moorland plateau are sparse in the published literature, and that even well-known species are worth documenting with specific location and date. He suggested continuing the survey into autumn to capture any late-season specimens.
